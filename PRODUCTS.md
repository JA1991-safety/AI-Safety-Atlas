# Products

Everything made for the AI Safety Products project, in one place. Each folder holds one product and a short README. The pages are self-contained HTML, so they open straight from the folder or from GitHub Pages.

| Product | Folder | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Common Ground: A Global Look at AI Safety | repository root (`src/`, `content/`, `editorial/`) | The atlas site with its interactive globe and country profiles. The code and pilot profiles are in the open pull requests #1 to #15 and are still in progress. The plan is in [`editorial/execution-plan.md`](editorial/execution-plan.md). |
| I Give a F*** About | [`i-give-a-f-about/`](i-give-a-f-about/) | AI safety career finder: pick the risks you care about and find the organisations working on them. |
| The Quiet Handover | [`gd-timeline/`](gd-timeline/) | Interactive timeline of a gradual disempowerment scenario, with its working list of decision points. |
| The Reasonable Path | [`gradual-disempowerment-simulator/`](gradual-disempowerment-simulator/) | Simulator of the world you shape as each reasonable choice hands a little more over to AI. |
| AI safety needs an operating system | [`operations-explainer/`](operations-explainer/) | Operations context for AI safety founders: what operations is and what to hire for. Version 2, plus the earlier Operations Field Map mockup. |
| Beyond Survival | [`good-for-humanity-research/`](good-for-humanity-research/) | Research review of what "good for humanity" means for alignment, across four literatures. Web page, Markdown and Word versions. |
