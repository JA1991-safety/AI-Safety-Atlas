# Beyond Survival

A research review of what "good for humanity" means for alignment, drawing on AI safety, philosophy of the good, the psychology of sustainable happiness, and frameworks for breaking down a human life.

## Files

The same review in three formats:

- `research-review.html`: the web page.
- `research-review.md`: Markdown, for editing.
- `beyond-survival-research-review.docx`: Word document.
